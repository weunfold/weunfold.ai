# Mark

The we unfold mark, drawn from its formulas in one of three hypotheses: `seeds` (A · Golden Seeds), `circles` (C · Golden Circles · Fade, the default) and `swirl` (D · Fibonacci Swirl).

- Provide `variant`; omit it to get the current default, `circles`.
- Provide `size` in px. Below 96 px the mark switches to its small cut by itself (fewer seeds, thicker rings); pass `small` only to force it.
- Provide `ink` for one-colour use: `var(--ivory)` on dark grounds, `var(--ink)` on light grounds and in print. Leave it out for colour.
- Do not recolour single seeds, rings or arms, add yellow, outline, shadow, stretch or rotate the mark (rotation is allowed only as motion).
- Keep clear space of at least the mark's radius divided by φ on every side. Smallest size: 20 px (D: 16 px).
- For a picture file use the `Marks` assets instead of a screenshot.
